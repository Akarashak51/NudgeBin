Nudge Bin: virtual prototype

A retrofit kit that thanks people who use the bin, nudges people who miss it, and redirects them when it is full. Press the buttons to see the same logic the ESP32 would run.

🔊Speaker is silent. Choose a scenario below.

DFPlayer speakerSilent

Decision rules

  1. Beam broken while someone is near: play a thank-you.
  2. Someone stays 3 s, beam never broken: play a gentle warning.
  3. Someone passes in under 3 s: stay silent.
  4. Fill at 85% or more: tell people the nearest free bin, using neighbour status.
